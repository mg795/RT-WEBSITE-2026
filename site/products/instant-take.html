HOW IT WORKS

Send it in.
We build it out.

"The turnaround is fast. I send something Monday and it's ready to review by Wednesday."

  • 1. Send the insight, Drop it in Telegram, email it, share a Drive link, or add it to your intake folder — whichever fits your workflow.
  • 2. We build the piece, Our team turns your insight into a fully structured, SEO and AEO-optimized article anchored in your brand voice and buyer intent.

QUALITY CONTROL

Every piece
earns its place.

"Nothing goes out unless it passes. That matters to us."

Every Instant Take™ article is scored across five dimensions before it reaches you: Content Substance, Client Voice Integrity, Tone Alignment, Topic Relevance, and Publishability. A score of 4.0 to 5.0 earns a PASS and ships. A score of 3.5 to 3.9 earns a Liberty Flag — we note the deviation and you decide. Anything below 3.5 bounces back for a rebuild. You only receive content that clears the bar.
